### 2.2 How We Work — `[NEW, placed on the Services page, above or beside the six-step process]`

> **We tell you what you need to know, when you need to know it — not everything at once.**
> Say you commission a site for a car dealership. Phase one is design — we ask about how it should look and feel from the customer's side. While that's happening, we're already gathering everything we'll need on operations from your staff — but we don't bring it up yet. Once the customer-facing side is signed off, we shift to operations. After that, email, mailing lists, CMS. Every feature is already scoped on day one — we just don't hand it to a department before it's relevant. Less noise, fewer decisions at once, a calmer build.

### 2.3 The six-step process — narrative text for each step `[NEW — replaces the bare pin-card labels]`

> **A six-step climb, not a checklist.**
> Same journey, every partner, every time — from the first "what if" conversation to the moment your product is live and someone's actually watching it.

**01 · Discovery**
We ask about the business first, not the software. What does this actually need to do, who's it for, and what happens if we get it wrong? Half the value here is us telling you what you *don't* need yet.

**02 · Planning**
We scope the real build — every phase is mapped from day one, even the parts we won't mention to you until they're relevant. You get a plan, not a guess.

**03 · Design**
Interfaces shaped around how the thing will actually be used, not a moodboard. If it's for someone standing at a counter with a customer waiting, it looks nothing like a dashboard built for someone at a desk — and we design accordingly.

**04 · Development**
Code you can hand off. Typed, tested, and reviewed — we write it so the next engineer, yours or ours, thanks us instead of rewriting it from scratch.

**05 · Testing**
We break it before your customers do. Edge cases, bad connections, the fields people fill in wrong — tested against how people actually behave, not how the spec says they should.

**06 · Launch**
We stay on the call. Deploy platform, CI/CD, content migration, gradual rollout — whatever's still shaky on launch day, we're the ones on it, not a support ticket queue.
